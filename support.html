A barcode found nothing

342,713 foods ship inside the app, and when a packet is not among them Keeping Tabs asks Open Food Facts. If neither has it, send us the packet's name and the barcode number and we will add it. A photograph of the nutrition panel helps.

A label read back the wrong numbers

The commonest error is a decimal point lost off small print, which costs a factor of ten. The app checks the macros against the printed energy and says which figure disagrees. If it read something wrong, every number on the screen can be corrected by hand before you save it.

Getting your data out

You → Your data → Export everything, as CSV or JSON. Every entry you have logged, with the nutrients that were actually declared. A blank cell means the source did not say, which is not the same as zero.

Deleting things

Your diary is on your phone and is never uploaded. You → Erase everything on this phone removes the diary, your weights, your goals and your profile. That is separate from your account: You → Account → Delete account removes the account itself and your emailing answer from our records.

Restoring a purchase

Keeping Tabs Complete is bought once and kept. On a new phone, sign in with the same Apple Account and use Restore purchases on the Complete screen.
